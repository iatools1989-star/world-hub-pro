# The ATS Resume Cheat Sheet
### 12-Point Formatting Checklist + Bullet Formula That Gets You Read

Texto de introdução (fonte Inter, 2-3 linhas):
> Up to 75% of resumes are rejected by applicant tracking software before a human ever sees them. Run every resume through this checklist before you apply — it takes ten minutes and works for entry-level, graduate, and career-change applications.

---

### Seção 1 — The 12-Point ATS Checklist
(estilo checklist com caixas quadradas em branco ao lado de cada item; título em azul-marinho)

1. ☐ Saved and submitted as a PDF (unless the posting explicitly asks for Word)
2. ☐ One page only for entry-level and under 10 years of experience
3. ☐ Standard section headings: "Experience," "Education," "Skills" — no creative names
4. ☐ No tables, text boxes, columns with graphics, icons, or photos
5. ☐ Standard fonts only: Arial, Calibri, Garamond, or Helvetica, size 10–12
6. ☐ Margins between 0.5 and 1 inch; consistent line spacing of 1.0–1.15
7. ☐ Full job-title keywords copied word-for-word from the job description
8. ☐ Contact info in the header: name, professional email, phone, city/state, LinkedIn URL
9. ☐ No street address, photo, age, date of birth, or marital status
10. ☐ File named "First Name Last Name Resume 2026.pdf"
11. ☐ Every skill listed is one you can explain in an interview
12. ☐ Proofread twice — once with a grammar tool, once by another human

---

### Seção 2 — The Bullet Formula (destaque em faixa amarela)

## ACTION VERB + TASK + QUANTIFIED RESULT

Texto explicativo curto:
> Every experience bullet — paid job, volunteer work, club, sports, or personal project — follows this formula. Numbers make claims believable.

Três exemplos (versão fraca riscada em vermelho; versão forte com check verde):

| ❌ Weak | ✅ Strong |
|---|---|
| Helped with social media | Created and scheduled 12 weekly Instagram posts for a student club, growing followers from 200 to 900 in one semester |
| Worked at a restaurant | Served 80+ customers per shift and trained 4 new hires on the POS system, cutting order errors by 15% |
| Good with people | Resolved an average of 25 customer requests daily, maintaining a 96% positive feedback rating over 6 months |

## PÁGINA 2

### Seção 3 — 20 Action Verbs That Replace "Responsible For"
(organizar em 6 grupos com ícones simples e monocromáticos)

- **Led:** Organized · Coordinated · Managed · Directed
- **Built:** Designed · Developed · Created · Launched
- **Grew:** Increased · Raised · Expanded · Grew
- **Analyzed:** Evaluated · Researched · Tracked · Measured
- **Improved:** Streamlined · Optimized · Reduced · Automated
- **Supported:** Trained · Mentored · Volunteered · Assisted

Nota de rodapé da seção (itálico):
> Use past tense for finished work, present tense only for your current role. Never repeat the same verb twice in a row.

---

### Seção 4 — The 5 Rejection Triggers (X vermelho em cada item)

1. **Keyword mismatch** — your skills don't use the exact words in the job description
2. **Graphic-heavy layout** — tables and icons turn your resume into unreadable text for the parser
3. **Generic soft skills** — "hard worker" and "team player" with zero evidence
4. **Two+ pages at entry level** — recruiters spend about 7 seconds on the first scan
5. **Typos and unprofessional details** — party-style email addresses, nicknames, or voicemail greetings

---

### Seção 5 — The 5-Minute Keyword Method (box azul-marinho com texto branco)

1. Open the job description and highlight every hard skill, tool, and certification
2. Copy the exact wording into your skills section (only the skills you truly have)
3. Weave the top three keywords naturally into your professional summary
4. Add one matching keyword per experience bullet where it honestly fits
5. Re-run this process for every new application — one generic resume fails every ATS

## Especificações de design (resumo para o Canva)

- Formato: US Letter, 2 páginas, margem de 0,6 pol.
- Títulos de seção: Montserrat ExtraBold, azul-marinho, 18-22 pt
- Corpo: Inter, 10,5-11 pt, cinza-escuro `#1F2937`
- Destaques: faixas amarelas com texto preto; nunca texto amarelo sobre branco
- Ícones: apenas uma família de ícones, linha fina, na cor azul-marinho (nada colorido)
- Exportar: PDF Standard, nome do arquivo `ATS-Resume-Cheat-Sheet.pdf`
- Após exportar, teste você mesmo: abra o PDF, selecione todo o texto e copie para o Bloco de Notas. Se o texto sair na ordem correta e sem símbolos quebrados, o ATS também vai lê-lo corretamente.
